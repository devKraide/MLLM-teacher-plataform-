# Arte inicial — origem e prompts

Imagens criadas pela ferramenta integrada Imagegen, sem CLI ou execução de modelos locais. Os arquivos usados pela aplicação estão no próprio projeto; os originais gerados foram preservados. A imagem de floresta enviada pelo usuário foi usada somente como referência de estilo.

- `public/assets/campus-v1.png`: 1536 × 1024, fundo composto inicial para direção visual e primeira navegação. Ainda não é um conjunto de tiles modulares ou camadas de oclusão.
- `public/assets/student-v1.png`: 1199 × 1312, PNG RGBA, avatar estático inicial com transparência. Animações e vistas direcionais ficam para refinamento.

## Prompt do cenário

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a high-quality actual 2D pixel-art game environment background for a small futuristic language school, in the detailed top-down RPG pixel art style of the provided forest reference (reference for style only; do not copy its layout). Landscape 1536 by 1024 composition. Orthographic top-down with shallow visible wall fronts, NOT isometric, no perspective vanishing point. Entire small campus visible. Very crisp pixel edges and rich clustered pixel texture, mature teal-green trees and intricate flowers like the reference, no blurry painterly gradients. The campus has a lush garden in the lower half with curved light stone paths and a small turquoise pond on the left, dense vegetation around the outer perimeter, park benches and low cyan glowing pathway bollards. The upper half contains an elegant small school seen as an open roof cutaway: a reception room in the upper-left center and ONE spacious language classroom in the upper-right center, connected by a broad walkable doorway in their dividing wall. Reception: cream terrazzo floor, compact welcome desk against the north wall, two lounge sofas, planter, cyan wall display. Classroom: warm pale floor, six individual desks arranged with generous walkable aisles, pale chairs, a large cyan wall-mounted board at the north wall, a small teacher station. Each interior has a clearly open wide entrance in its south wall leading directly to the garden path. Architecture is ivory and muted teal with subtle cyan technological accents, welcoming biophilic futurism, not cyberpunk. Keep clear empty walkable floor and path areas. Midday light, subtle warm highlights and soft pixel shadows. All rooms and paths align to a consistent 32px game grid and coherent human scale. No characters, no people, no logos, no text, no UI, no border. This image will be used for the first playable art-direction slice, so prioritize legible connected navigation and consistent top-down geometry. Trees frame the map but do not hide doorways. Buildings should occupy approximately x=280..1330 and y=100..520; lower garden y=550..960. Keep the bottom-central entry path open.

## Prompt do avatar

Create a single game-ready character sprite on a truly transparent background. Detailed crisp pixel art for a top-down 2D role-playing game set in a welcoming futuristic language school. A friendly young adult student with short dark brown hair, warm medium skin, muted teal jacket with a small cream panel, dark charcoal trousers, white sneakers and small tan backpack straps. Front-facing three-quarter TOP-DOWN RPG view, slightly see the top of the head, entire body visible, standing neutral arms at sides. Compact 3-head-tall proportions; clean readable silhouette. Soft pixel shading, clustered pixels, no smooth gradients. One character only, centered, occupying about 80 percent of image height; no background, no floor, no cast shadow, no text, no props, no frame. Palette matches cream architecture, teal plants and cyan accents. This is a usable static avatar sprite, not a concept sheet.
